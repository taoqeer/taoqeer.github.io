I'm a Software Engineer II at JPMorgan Chase in Bangalore, working on enterprise CI/CD infrastructure and software supply chain security. I lead a team of 6 engineers and spend most of my time thinking about how to make developers faster and software delivery more secure.

Before JPMC, I spent 4 years at EY building backend systems — invoice processing platforms at massive scale, GenAI document pipelines, and event-driven architectures. That's where I learned that performance problems are almost always in places you don't expect, and that profiling before optimizing is the only honest way to work.

I started my career in cybersecurity at PwC, doing ITGC audits and secure SDLC work. That early grounding in security thinking shapes how I approach software today — especially now that I'm building supply chain security infrastructure.

Outside of work I solve DSA problems obsessively — 500+ across LeetCode, Codeforces, and CodeChef. I find competitive programming genuinely fun, not just useful. I'm also slowly building out open source projects that demonstrate the kind of systems I work on professionally but can't share.

I graduated from University of Engineering & Management, Kolkata with a B.Tech in Computer Science (CGPA 8.97) in 2021.
